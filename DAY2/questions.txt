Practice Programs (Easy → Hard)

Level 1 — Easy

1. Student Information
    * Declare variables for name, age, USN, branch, and CGPA, then display them.
2. Basic Arithmetic Calculator
    * Take two numbers and display their sum, difference, product, quotient, and remainder.
3. Area & Perimeter of a Rectangle
    * Read length and breadth, then calculate area and perimeter.

⸻

Level 2 — Medium

4. Temperature Converter
    * Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit and Fahrenheit to Celsius.
5. Simple Interest Calculator
    * Input principal, rate, and time, then calculate simple interest and total amount.
6. Swap Two Numbers
    * Perform swapping once using a temporary variable and once without using a temporary variable.

⸻

Level 3 — Intermediate

7. Type Casting Demonstration
    * Show implicit (widening) and explicit (narrowing) type casting with suitable examples, and observe the changes in values.
8. Marks Percentage Calculator
    * Read marks for five subjects, calculate the total, average, and percentage.
9. Salary Breakdown
    * Given a basic salary, calculate HRA, DA, gross salary, and net salary using fixed percentage values.

⸻

Level 4 — Challenging

10. Operator Demonstration Program
    * Write a program that demonstrates arithmetic, assignment, unary, relational, logical, and ternary operators using appropriate examples and displays the result of each operation.
11. Expression Evaluation
    * Create a program that evaluates a complex mathematical expression involving multiple operators and verify the effect of operator precedence. Use parentheses to compare different evaluation orders.
12. Mini Student Result Calculator
    * Read a student’s details and marks, calculate total, average, percentage, determine pass/fail based on a chosen passing criterion, and display the complete result in a well-formatted output.
